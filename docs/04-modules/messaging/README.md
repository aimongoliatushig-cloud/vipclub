# Messaging and Feedback Module

## Purpose

Own internal direct messaging, contextual task/project discussion, upward employee feedback, recipient-anonymous submissions, PWA notifications, consented customer communications, delivery state, and audit.

## Internal communication

Authorized entertainers and employees may communicate with teammates, Lead Entertainer, Branch Manager, General Manager, CEO, and other permitted users. Organizational and branch scope apply.

Task/project discussion remains attached to its work context rather than being lost in unrelated messaging.

## Upward feedback and recipient anonymity

An employee may select an authorized recipient for a concern or incident-related submission. Where policy permits, the sender may be hidden from the normal recipient.

Recipient-anonymous does not mean technically untraceable. Tightly permissioned CEO/audit authority may reveal the sender for an approved purpose, and every reveal is audited. Product wording must disclose this accurately.

## Notifications

Notifications cover tasks, deadlines, approvals, entertainer requests, goal progress, reconciliation exceptions, and other approved events. Timing, escalation, recipient, branch timezone, and throttling are configurable.

## Customer communications

Campaigns and point/privilege messages require valid consent and approved channel preferences. Internal messaging permissions do not grant customer campaign access.
