# Delivery Roadmap

The VIP Club system is planned in four phases. The first three phases are now outlined.

## Phase 1 — Foundation Operations

### HR and entertainer operations

- Record entertainer attendance.
- Calculate attendance penalties.
- Calculate entertainer profit share.

### Customer onboarding

- Hosts or receptionists check whether a visitor already has a member profile.
- If no profile exists, the host creates one.
- The host records marketing-consent preferences.
- Supported promotional-message channels: Viber, Telegram, and email.

## Phase 2 — Workforce Task Management

- Support four branches and their teams.
- The CEO can assign tasks across the company.
- Branch managers can assign tasks within their own branch and team.
- Assignees receive task notifications.
- Tasks keep comments and conversations.
- Assignees can mark tasks complete and record execution results.
- Completion can include images and execution notes.
- Dashboards show task status, deadlines, and completion statistics.

## Phase 3 — CRM and Customer Intelligence

- Capture customer relationship information.
- Analyze reservations, including entertainer preferences.
- Measure customer loyalty.
- Assign members to one of five membership levels based on a confirmed spend-based metric.

## Phase 4

- **Open question:** Scope, outcomes, dependencies, owners, and success measures have not yet been defined.
