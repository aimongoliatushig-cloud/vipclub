# Unified Delivery Roadmap

This roadmap merges the detailed ERPNext/Frappe knowledge package with the priority sequence confirmed in the VIP Club project conversations. GitHub Markdown is the business source of truth; Linear tracks delivery work.

## Phase 0 — Discovery and Platform Foundation

- Audit the existing repository, ERPNext/Frappe version, custom apps, infrastructure, integrations, and current documentation.
- Confirm company and four-branch model, source systems, data ownership, user roles, and legal/financial constraints.
- Establish policy versioning, role and branch permissions, audit logging, data migration, integration, and test foundations.
- Produce the canonical documentation tree, process inventory, data dictionary, architecture mapping, and decision register.

## Phase 1 — Foundation Operations and Customer Onboarding

- Entertainer and staff identity, branch assignment, scheduling, attendance, lateness/no-show handling, and approved penalties.
- Entertainer rank, income, three-day settlement, and controlled performance-based loan vertical slice.
- Customer registration by host or reception, duplicate checking, profile creation, marketing consent, and preferred channels.
- Reservations and verified attribution required for reliable customer and entertainer data.

## Phase 2 — Workforce Task Management

- Four-branch organizational structure, teams, and role-based assignment.
- CEO company-wide assignment; managers assign within their branch and team.
- Task acknowledgement, status, discussions, comments, attachments, completion evidence, rework, approvals, and audit history.
- Notifications, deadline escalation, dashboards, and individual/manager completion statistics.
- Formal orders, policy acknowledgement, maintenance, feedback, complaints, and retention workflows extend this phase as capacity allows.

## Phase 3 — CRM, Loyalty, Benefits, and Customer Intelligence

- Customer 360 profiles, visits, reservations, spend, entertainer affinity, segments, and loyalty analytics.
- Five-level membership program with branch-specific configurable thresholds.
- Benefits and privilege management, benefit-use tracking, and an auditable cashback ledger.
- Consent-based rich-media campaigns with preferred channels, communication history, opt-outs, and campaign outcomes.
- Customer loyalty, value, visit-cadence, and lapsed-member insights.
- Before implementation, confirm membership formula, cross-branch rules, thresholds, points value, expiry, redemption controls, and privacy rules.

## Phase 4 — Goal Engine and Management Intelligence

- CEO-manager monthly planning loop.
- AI-assisted, explainable target proposals and daily/weekly operational reviews.
- Manager actions, risk alerts, accountability, executive dashboards, and approval boundaries.

## Phase 5 — Finance, Compliance, and Production Readiness

- Payroll and statutory rules after legal validation.
- Accounting reconciliation, E-Barimt evidence, approved bank/payment integration, and operational controls.
- Reporting hardening, security review, pilot, training, support, backup/recovery, and production rollout.

## Phase 6 — Cross-Branch Optimization

- Customer transfer, capacity and availability optimization.
- Internal entertainer transfers and referral/commission attribution.
- Forecasting and approved AI recommendations.

## Reconciliation principles

- The package's detailed entertainer vertical slice is preserved inside Phase 1.
- The conversation's workforce task-management priority remains Phase 2.
- The package's customer core and loyalty capabilities are consolidated into Phase 3 to avoid splitting dependent data and policy work.
- The Goal Engine follows the operational data foundations in Phase 4.
- Legal, tax, policy, integration, and data-retention details remain open decisions until formally approved.
