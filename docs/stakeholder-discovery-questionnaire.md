# Stakeholder Discovery Questionnaire

Use these questions in structured interviews. Record each answer in the stakeholder clarification register with an owner, status, approval date, and affected documents.

## CEO and owner

1. What are the four most important business outcomes for the next 12 months?
2. What must be visible on the CEO dashboard every day, week, and month?
3. Which decisions require CEO approval: loans, payroll, payments, sales goals, loyalty changes, high-value benefits, policy changes, and branch setup?
4. What financial approval limits apply to each role?
5. What are the official company and branch structures, and which branches can share customers, staff, and reporting?
6. What are the five membership levels, their customer promise, and their benefits?
7. What is the approved sales-target baseline and required improvement percentage?
8. Which activities should Hermes recommend, remind, summarize, or never influence?
9. What pilot branch, user groups, rollout timing, and success measures should be used?
10. Which rules are non-negotiable for customer privacy, employee fairness, and financial control?

## Branch managers and sales managers

1. What daily, weekly, and monthly branch decisions do you make?
2. Which sales data do you trust today, and where is it stored?
3. How do you build a monthly target and action plan today?
4. What actions most reliably improve sales, visits, reservations, and repeat customers?
5. What task states, deadlines, evidence, and escalation rules are actually needed?
6. Which roles can assign work within a branch, and which work requires CEO visibility?
7. What are the most common attendance, staffing, and maintenance problems?
8. What customer and CRM insights would change your decisions?
9. When a branch is full, how should customer transfer and attribution work?
10. What settings should be branch-specific versus company-wide?

## Lead entertainers and entertainers

1. What does good entertainer performance mean in daily practice?
2. Which attendance, reservation, revenue, loyalty, training, and conduct measures are fair for rank evaluation?
3. What evidence is reliable for customer affinity and repeat visits?
4. What should entertainers see in their PWA each day?
5. How should rank progress, benefits, and missing requirements be explained?
6. What sales or income events should count toward a three-day settlement?
7. Which penalties need evidence, notice, correction, and appeal?
8. What loan request, repayment, and balance information must be visible?
9. How should temporary or permanent branch transfer work?
10. What alerts, tasks, or standards should a lead entertainer be able to manage?

## Human resources manager

1. Define the three employment types and the official contracts for each.
2. What documents, checks, training, and acknowledgements are required before activation?
3. What employee lifecycle states and offboarding steps are required?
4. How are lateness, absence, no-show, leave, and attendance corrections defined?
5. What penalty rules are legally approved and how can they be appealed?
6. What does **гарааны торгууль** mean in business practice? Define its trigger, amount/formula, evidence, approval, correction, and appeal process.
6. What data is private to HR and unavailable to branch managers?
8. What survey, feedback, complaint, and anti-retaliation process is required?
9. What retention or resignation-risk indicators may be used?
10. How are internal transfers approved and tracked?
11. What staff training and policy acknowledgements are mandatory?

## General accountant and payroll accountant

1. Confirm entertainer settlement frequency, period boundaries, and income-share formulas.
2. Confirm the two monthly payroll dates for day-based and fixed-salary workers.
3. Define extra-day, overtime, leave, penalty, deduction, and adjustment calculations.
4. What source records are authoritative for sales, attendance, loans, and payroll?
5. Who can prepare, review, approve, post, and correct a pay run?
6. How do payroll and settlement corrections work after approval or payment?
7. Which accounting records, reports, tax, and E-Barimt evidence are required?
8. What bank-account verification and change-control process is required?
9. How should payment failures, reversals, and partial bank-batch results be reconciled?
10. What financial reports must be available by branch, employment type, and period?

## Transaction accountant and payment accountant

1. What transactions are entered manually versus imported?
2. What evidence must be attached to each transaction, settlement, adjustment, and payment?
3. What reconciliations are completed daily, every three days, twice monthly, and monthly?
4. Who may initiate a corporate bank payment batch and who must approve it?
5. Does the bank require a second approval in its own portal?
6. What bank-gateway responses, errors, and reconciliation files are available?
7. How are failed, duplicate, cancelled, or delayed payments handled?
8. What access should be masked or restricted for bank accounts and payment data?
9. How are supplier, customer, and employee payment records distinguished?
10. What audit export is required for management or external review?

## Marketing and content manager

1. What customer segments are useful today and what criteria define them?
2. Which campaigns, promotions, events, and benefits are planned?
3. What customer consent wording and channels are permitted?
4. Which customer messages need managerial or CEO approval?
5. What campaign outcomes matter: delivery, visit, reservation, spend, or redemption?
6. What media formats and content-review rules are required?
7. How should opt-outs, preference changes, and communication history work?
8. What reports should show campaign and segment performance?

## Reception, hosts, servers, bartenders, security, and driver

1. What is the fastest safe customer-registration flow at the point of service?
2. What data must be captured, optional, or hidden?
3. What reservation and arrival information is needed to provide service?
4. How should benefits and cashback be verified at redemption?
5. What branch task, shift, maintenance, or incident notifications are needed in the PWA?
6. What evidence is required when service, maintenance, or incident work is completed?
7. What are the safe-transport eligibility, consent, trip-record, and privacy rules?
8. What should happen when the system is unavailable or network connectivity is poor?

## Technical owner and integration owners

1. What ERPNext/Frappe versions, custom apps, sites, databases, and deployment methods are in use?
2. What data exists in POS, attendance, reservations, banking, and messaging systems?
3. Which APIs, file exports, sandbox environments, credentials, and rate limits are available?
4. How are identifiers matched across systems?
5. What backup, recovery, monitoring, log retention, and incident-response standards apply?
6. What is the planned PWA frontend technology and authentication approach?
7. What CI, test, migration, release, and rollback workflow is available?
8. What security review, encryption, secret-management, and access-review controls are required?
